The **makhārij** (مخارج الحروف) are the points of articulation: the precise places where each letter takes its sound. Tajweed books do not divide them in exactly the same way, but the same main zones come back again and again.

## The main zones

- **The throat**: several guttural letters, such as the ʿayn and the ḥāʾ.
- **The tongue**: most letters, depending on which part of the tongue touches the point of articulation.
- **The lips**: the bāʾ, the mīm and the wāw.

## A simple method

1. Say the letter alone, slowly, with a short vowel.
2. Place the tongue or the lips in the right spot, then hold that position.
3. Compare with a recognised reciter, then record yourself.
4. Repeat each letter inside three or four words.

## A common mistake

Mixing up the ṣād, the sīn and the zāy, or the ḍād and the ḍāl, is very common. Correcting these pairs one letter at a time moves you forward faster than reviewing the madd rules again.
